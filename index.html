Metal Soldier: Time War

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Android game Metal Soldier: Time War handles information. Android package name: com.metalsoldier.timewar.

Current offline build. The game does not require an account and does not contain ads, real-money purchases, subscriptions, analytics, or tracking. The sections about advertising and billing below describe planned features and do not apply to this build.

Game information on your device

The game stores progress and unlocked levels, weapons and upgrades, earned in-game currency, daily rewards, and settings in its local Android app storage. It uses these records to resume your progress and remember your preferences. In the current build, this information is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Game saves are kept locally so you can resume play. To delete them, go to Android Settings → Apps → Metal Soldier: Time War → Storage → Clear storage or uninstall the game. Clearing storage removes your saved progress and settings. The current build has no game account or server copy of your game progress for the developer to delete.

Advertising in future versions

This section will apply only to versions that include ads. Depending on the ad service and its configuration, the service may receive information such as your IP address, an approximate location derived from it, device or app identifiers (including an advertising ID where available), ad views or interactions, and diagnostic information. An ad service may use this information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.

If Google AdMob is enabled, see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Any other advertising providers, the purposes they use data for, and available choices will be identified here before the relevant version is released. If the version offers an in-game ad privacy control, you can use it to manage your choices. You can also manage the device advertising ID in Android settings where supported.

Optional purchases and subscriptions in future versions

This section will apply only when real-money in-game items or subscriptions become available. Purchases made through Google Play are processed by Google Play. The game does not request your full payment card details. To grant or restore supported items or premium benefits, the game may receive information from Google Play such as the product ID, purchase token, transaction status, and subscription status. If the game uses a verification service, this policy will identify what it receives, how it uses and protects the information, and how long it keeps it before the service is used.

For information about how Google handles payment and account data, see the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play Subscriptions using the account that bought it. Clearing game storage or uninstalling the game does not cancel a subscription.

Privacy questions and support

If you email us, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. This is used to answer and follow up on your request. Support messages are retained only as needed to address the request or meet applicable legal obligations. You can use the same contact to request deletion of your support correspondence. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.
